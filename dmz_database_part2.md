# Call of Duty: MW4 DMZ — Master Database (Part 2)

## 5. Map Intel: The Hajin Exclusion Zone
Deployments drop squads directly into the Hajin Exclusion Zone, a highly volatile region containing varied architectural sectors, high-density industrial plants, and secret underground bunkers.

### Match Timer & Environmental Hazards
* The 25-Minute Limit: Matches feature a strict 25-minute countdown timer.
* The Expanding Storm: When the timer hits zero, a localized radiation storm begins expanding from a randomized core point on the map, contaminating extraction zones one by one.

### Regional Breakdown
* The Northern Frontier: Dominated by dense forestry, rural farming hamlets, and rocky ridges. Cover is primarily natural, making long-range optics and Scout tracking traits incredibly potent.
* The Southern Industrial Corridor: A concrete urban zone filled with high-rise construction projects, dense warehouses, shipping yards, and complex multi-level interiors. This area favors close-quarters weaponry and Warfighter combat perks.
* Subterranean Hardpoints: Scattered across Hajin are high-threat underground military complexes. These zones feature tight bottlenecks, a high concentration of Tier 3 AI forces, and contain the most lucrative weapon manuals.

## 6. Enemy Ranks & Boss Strategies
The AI forces occupying Hajin are organized by distinct visual armor tiers, denoting their health pools and tactical behavior.

### AI Threat Matrix
* Tier 1 (Militia Forces): Light clothing, basic chest rigs. They utilize un-suppressed, low-attachment weapons and feature poor aim at distance.
* Tier 2 (Regular Army): Full military fatigues, basic ballistic helmets, and light armor plates. They communicate effectively and utilize basic cover flanking maneuvers.
* Tier 3 (Heavy Shock Troops): Fully encapsulated in heavy EOD ballistic armor and reinforced helmets. They are highly aggressive and use heavy suppressive light machine guns.

### Named Boss Targets & Strategic Breakdowns

#### Boss 1: Gorani (The Forest Phantom)
* Location: Found roaming the dense foliage and hidden logging camps of the Northern Frontier.
* Behavior: Gorani utilizes active camouflage ghillie suits and fires a high-caliber suppressed sniper rifle. He moves constantly after taking damage and leaves tripwire mines along his escape routes.
* Strategy: Use a Scout Build with Operator Tracker to visually highlight his physical movement trails through the brush. Use smoke grenades to block his sightlines, close the distance rapidly, and use high fire-rate weapons to overwhelm his light armor.

#### Boss 2: The Warden
* Location: Anchored inside the heavily fortified Central Prison Complex.
* Behavior: Heavily armored and accompanied by a continuous squad of Tier 3 Shock Troops. The Warden utilizes a heavy riot shield with an integrated flashbulb system to blind rushing players while firing a fully automatic shotgun.
* Strategy: Bring dedicated tactical gear. Use thermite or drill charges directly on his riot shield to force a stagger animation that exposes his back. While he is staggered, support players must focus fire on his exposed helmet.

#### Boss 3: The Colossus
* Location: Guards the deep underground industrial vaults of the Southern Corridor.
* Behavior: A massive, slow-moving juggernaut armor build wielding a devastating hand-held Minigun. He features an immense health pool and uses heavy incendiary grenades to burn down player cover.
* Strategy: Never challenge The Colossus in a direct hallway line-of-sight. Utilize the environment to break engagement ranges. Have one player pull his agro around tight pillars while teammates deploy armor-piercing ammunition from his flanks.

#### Boss 4: The Deathstalker
* Location: Appears dynamically under the cover of night or within pitch-black subterranean installations.
* Behavior: A highly mobile assassin unit utilizing advanced night-vision technology, smoke screens, and dual suppressed submachine guns. He strikes from the dark and vanishes into smoke lines when targeted.
* Strategy: Ensure at least two squad members are equipped with Thermal Optics or have the Viper Sense trait active to counter his smoke screens. Deploy portable trophy systems to swallow his smoke canisters.

#### Global Threat: The Roaming Battle Tank
* Location: Follows main paved transit highways looping around the center of the map.
* Behavior: A fully operational, heavily armored combat vehicle that automatically targets any infantry or unarmored player vehicles within a wide perimeter radius using its main cannon.
* Strategy: Avoid entirely unless equipped for anti-vehicle combat. Use heavy launcher equipment (RPGs/Javelins) or coordinate heavy thermite drops directly onto its rear engine ventilation bays to disable its mobility.

## 7. Weapons, Gunsmith & Crafting Economy
The weapon ecosystem balances on-the-fly extraction value with permanent technical modifications handled back at the base.

### Weapon Manuals & Blueprint Progression
* The Cache Loop: Scattered across highly defended strongholds are colored Weapon Caches. Extracting successfully with a Weapon Manual found inside these containers permanently expands your account's blueprint depth.
* The Gunsmith Station: Weapons can be customized with up to 9 attachment slots simultaneously. However, adding multiple attachments incurs significant cumulative cash costs at the workbench.

### The 3D Printer Economy
The 3D Printer converts basic scrap metal, electronic components, and chemicals brought home in your backpack into vital tactical gear:
* Armor Vests: Print specialized 2-plate or 3-plate variants (including specialized Medic or Comms configurations).
* Backpacks: Upgrade baseline single-slot capacity storage assets to Medium or Large footprints.
* Consumables: Craft specific keycards, self-revive stems, or radiation medication blocks using collected recipe schematics.

### Suppression & Sound Mechanics
* AI Alert Levels: Noise is a mechanical trigger in Hajin. Firing un-suppressed weapons increases the local AI alert state, causing nearby troops to take defensive cover and call in reinforcement helicopters or armored trucks.
* The Stealth Loop: Running a suppressor attachment is highly recommended for solo players or looting builds. Combined with the Scout tree's Stealthy trait, sound suppression allows operators to clear out objectives cleanly without triggering regional combat escalations.
