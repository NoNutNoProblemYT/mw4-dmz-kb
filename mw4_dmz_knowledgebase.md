# MW4 DMZ Reference Manual & AI Knowledge Base

## Section 1: System & Deployment Parameters
* **Official Launch Date:** October 23, 2026.
* **Early Access Matrix:** October 20, 2026 (Eligible digital pre-orders).
* **Distribution Framework:** Paid premium game mode included within Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 (Not Free-To-Play).
* **Deployment Zone:** Hajin Exclusion Zone.
* **Maximum Progression Cap:** Level 70.

## Section 2: Player & FOB Progression Loops
* **Forward Operating Base (FOB):** The primary structural hub where player upgrades, inventory infrastructure, and station modifications are processed.
* **Core Ingestion Loop:** Completing narrative missions, extracting localized loot, completing dynamic ops, and neutralizing hostile elements awards experience to scale the dedicated DMZ Player Level (1-70).
* **Operator Progression:** Features 18 distinct Dog Tag levels, 66 unlockable passive Traits, and dedicated Operator Prestige tiers.

## Section 3: Interface & Match Mechanics
* **HUD Telemetry Matrix:** Real-time data streams tracking Tac-Map orientation, match time limits, localized threat metrics, dynamic operational orders, squad status, cash reserves, backpack slots, and weapon ammunition profiles.
* **Threat Level Scaling:** Neutralizing local AI forces permanently scales the sector's localized threat rating. High-tier threat states trigger automated elite reinforcement drops via heavy choppers.
* **Exfiltration Framework:** Available extraction points are mapped dynamically per match instance and visually highlighted directly on the operator's Tac-Map interface.

## Section 4: Inventory, Loot, & Crafting Data
* **Default Spawn Loadout:** Operators deploy into Hajin equipped with a baseline parachute, armor plates, primary/secondary designated weapons, a self-revive kit, selected killstreaks, and tactical/lethal configurations.
* **Medium Backpack Attributes:** Expands inventory capacity slots, features a dedicated structural slot for a third stowed weapon, and incorporates a Secure Slot that fully protects items from being lost upon operator death.
* **3D Printer Fabrication:** Allows players to synthesize high-tier tactical gear and custom mission items inside the exclusion zone by utilizing collected Recipes, Blueprints, and Raw Resources.

## Section 5: Faction Operations & Threat Matrix
* **Lieutenant Hunts:** Mid-tier High-Value Target (HVT) contracts against heavily armored AI units. Completing these rewards localized cash multipliers, high-tier armor items, and player experience points.
* **Commander Encounters:** High-tier boss engagements against roaming AI entities carrying endgame weapon cases and materials. These bosses broadcast a massive tracking signature across the entire Tac-Map.
* **Hunt Operator Contracts:** Evaluates lobby telemetry to initiate a PvP dynamic operation, marking an enemy real-player squad's generalized coordinates on the Tac-Map. Eliminating target operators directly advances unique DMZ weapon camo challenges.
