# Call of Duty: MW4 DMZ — Master Database (Part 1)

## 1. The Forward Operating Base (FOB) & Station Unlocks
The Forward Operating Base (FOB) serves as your navigable, physical command center between deployments. Progression ranges across a standalone DMZ Player Level Track (Levels 1–70). Upgrades provide visual changes and direct attribute enhancements via the 3D Printer.

### Core Stations Available Immediately
* Operators Station: Manage active characters, allocate Trait Points, track MIA status, and trigger Operator Prestige.
* Stash / Loadout: Store extracted weapons, tools, consumables, and printer resources.
* DMZ Orders Menu: Track story-driven faction directives, dynamic map operations, and incoming reward streams.
* Firing Range: Test custom weapon calibrations and optics from close quarters out to long distances.
* Survival Kits Locker: Store and open themed resource bundles containing targeted medical, armor, or recovery supplies.
* Bounty Leaderboard: Displays active threat data tracking the Top 50 Most Wanted Operators and elite Bounty Hunters.
* Deploy Threshold: Head out to the military transport helicopter to begin your next deployment.

### Advanced Stations & Progression Unlock Requirements
* 3D Printer Station: Unlocks permanently at DMZ Player Level 2. Consumes looted materials to craft gear and install physical updates across all FOB systems.
* Boss Board: Unlocks immediately upon completing your second DMZ Order. Allows spending currency to purchase high-value lieutenant intel, map their strongholds, and track global Commanders.
* FOB Vendor Market: Unlocks permanently at DMZ Player Level 5. Features a daily rotating shop selling weapons, gear, and field upgrades for DMZ Cash. Can be physically upgraded via 3D Printer blueprints to add permanent purchase slots.
* Dog Tag Case: Unlocks via progression to proudly display collected trophies stripped from eliminated enemy Operators.
* DMZ Progression Station: Unlocks as a dedicated physical monument tracking your global career climb toward the Level 70 endgame.

## 2. Dog Tag Tiers & Progression Mechanics
An operator’s capability is directly tied to the rank of their persistent Dog Tag. Surving extractions steadily increases the tag tier, unlocking Trait Points at every individual sub-level up to a hard cap of 18 total points.

### The Tier Ladder
* Steel Tier: Levels 1 – 3 (Initial deployment rank)
* Bronze Tier: Levels 4 – 6
* Silver Tier: Levels 7 – 9
* Gold Tier: Levels 10 – 12
* Damascus Tier: Levels 13 – 15
* Onyx Tier: Levels 16 – 18 (Maximum build capacity)

### Death, MIA, and Re-Enlistment
If an active operator is KIA and your squad fails to extract them, they enter MIA Status.
* Gear Loss: The operator’s active backpack, armor vest, and personal loadout items are lost immediately.
* The Re-Enlistment Fine: To reactivate that specific character slot without wiping their hard-earned Dog Tag level, you must pay a premium Re-Enlistment Cash Fine at the FOB.
* Cost Reductions: Upgrading the Operator Station using the 3D Printer permanently decreases the cost of this MIA recovery fee.

### Operator Prestige System
Once an individual Operator reaches Onyx III (Level 18) and caps out their Trait Points, they qualify for Operator Prestige.
* The Reset: Executing a prestige entirely wipes that character's trait progression tree back to zero.
* The Reward: In exchange for the reset, the Operator receives a single Permanent Prestige Trait. This unique trait is selected randomly and remains permanently active on that specific character asset.

## 3. Active Duty Slot Optimization Builds
* Build 1 (The PvP Hunter): Focused on locating and neutralizing hostile extraction squads using the synergy of Viper Sense and Operator Tracker. Securing a kill triggers Quick Fix, while Killing Spree extends combat buffs.
* Build 2 (The High-Value Looter): Focused on breaking into high-tier locked spaces, securing gold items, and funding the global stash. Lockpicking grants free access into restricted rooms, while Lucky skews container probabilities. Extracted valuables gain a 20% bonus from Market Contracts.
* Build 3 (The Squad Anchor): Serving as the durable backbone of a three-man team during high-risk encounters. Revive Speed ensures rapid team recovery. Numb the Pain provides temporary defensive armor buffs immediately upon revival. If all drops fail, Survivor / Die Hard grants infinite bleed-out time and automatically summons an emergency extraction helicopter when crossing into an Exfil sector.

## 4. Complete Master Traits Index
The system features 66 total Traits split cleanly into three focus domains. Every tree houses 7 Major Traits and 15 hidden, randomized Minor Traits.

### Track A: Warfighter Tree (PvP & Combat Focus)
* Combat Resistance: Lowers incoming explosive and tactical equipment damage profiles.
* Killing Spree: Eliminating enemy operators extends the timer on all active combat buffs.
* LT Hunter: Increases the amount of tactical map intel harvested when hacking Lieutenant Towers.
* Possum / Pawsome: Once per match, if downed, allows an absolute self-revive straight into Last Stand with fully restored health chunks (Note: health will begin a slow, continuous drain afterward).
* Quick Fix: Instantly forces health regeneration to trigger upon confirming a kill against an AI combatant or enemy player.
* Viper Sense: Feeds spatial visual or acoustic warnings when hostile operators penetrate your immediate vicinity.

### Track B: Scout Tree (Looting & Intel Focus)
* Lockpicking: Grants the manual capability to attempt bypass mechanisms on locked standard doors without requiring a designated key.
* Lucky: Skews container drop probabilities to raise the spawn rate of tracking items needed for active missions.
* Market Contracts: Applies a flat +20% bonus valuation to all extracted valuables upon a successful match exit.
* Mini Tracker: Instantly casts a localized radar pulse the exact microsecond you open any previously unlooted crate.
* Objective Oriented: Extends the tactical detection radius of side operations present on your Tac-Map.
* Operator Tracker: Highlights the recent physical footprints left behind by enemy players on the terrain.
* Organized: Modifies inventory stacking, effectively doubling maximum item stack limits for printer components.
* Safe Tracker: Constantly pings and marks the location of the nearest locked map safe every few minutes.
* Scavenger Pro: Guarantees extra ammunition or armor plate drops from killed combatants, and doubles the reserve return from picking up ammo packs.
* Sensor Cam: Sends an active threat warning to your heads-up display if an enemy operator crosses into your monitored sensor zone.
* Stealthy: Visually suppresses your presence, slowing the speed at which AI suspicion stars accumulate when you are spotted.

### Track C: Survivalist Tree (Utility & Durability Focus)
* Metabolism: Grants a passive, slow health recovery that patches up lost individual health chunks over time.
* Numb the Pain: Infuses your operator with a temporary defensive painkiller armor buffer immediately upon a teammate revival.
* Paratrooper: Cushions vertical drops, minimizing fall damage profiles taken while under a deployed canopy.
* Plate Pouch: Permanently expands your base reserve armor plate storage capacity by +2 slots.
* Plate Speed: Accelerates the physical animation speed required to insert replacement armor plates into your vest.
* Popcorn Tacticals: Increases the item drop probability of AI units caught inside active tactical grenade areas.
* Quick Regen: Cuts down the delay timer, allowing baseline health recovery to begin faster after taking damage.
* Rescue Mission: Generates immediate bonus cash and resource distributions when redeploying or extracting down squadmates.
* Resource Hoarder: Expands master resource collection speed and lifts total inventory cap ceilings.
* Resource Management: Automatically diverts collected material overflow to replenish whichever resource category currently sits at your lowest value.
* Resupply: Causes ammo crates to spawn bonus munitions, while standard armor boxes provide +2 extra plates upon interaction.
* Revive Speed: Permanently increases the interaction speed when reviving yourself or bleeding squad members.
* Superimmune: Dampens damage ticks taken inside radiation or toxic gas clouds while extending the raw health pool of your equipped gas mask.
* Survivor / Die Hard: Mitigates down penalties by granting infinite bleed-out duration during Last Stand, increasing your down crawl speed, and triggering an automated extraction chopper call-in when crawling into an Exfil sector.
